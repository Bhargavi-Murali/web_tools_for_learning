Elastic Collision

Learning objectives:

1. Conservation of Momentum

2. Inertia: increase the mass of the ball to see how it resists motion.

The starting positions are symmetric about the center. The balls collide only when their chosen velocities make them approach each other, or when the rear ball catches the front ball. For large velocities, reduce animation speed if the collision happens too quickly.

Elastic-collision calculation
